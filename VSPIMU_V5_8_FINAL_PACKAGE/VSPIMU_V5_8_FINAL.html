Clinical screening boundary
This V5.8 page adds literature-informed screening metrics, onboard ESP32 analytics, independent phone reference analytics, and a doctor-shareable session report. It does not diagnose Parkinson’s disease, freezing of gait, tremor disorders, or any other medical condition. Freeze Index is a published spectral feature, but published performance varies and study-specific thresholds are not universally valid. Terrain classes such as stairs/up-slope/down-slope are deliberately not claimed in this single-shank V5.8 configuration.
