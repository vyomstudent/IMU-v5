Clinical screening boundary
This V6 page retains the existing phone-side gait engine and report. The ESP32-C3 transports raw IMU samples; the phone performs the available processing. Optional phone-IMU features are descriptive research measurements, not clinical diagnoses. It does not diagnose Parkinson’s disease, freezing of gait, tremor disorders, or any other medical condition. Freeze Index is a published spectral feature, but published performance varies and study-specific thresholds are not universally valid. Terrain classes such as stairs/up-slope/down-slope are deliberately not claimed in this single-shank V6 configuration.
